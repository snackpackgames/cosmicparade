# Glazes come from bond-gated per-entity ability lists

Each entity owns a tiered list of glazes. Giving divine gifts raises bond and unlocks
higher tiers, and the player chooses which unlocked glaze to apply when firing a tile.
This follows the design's own framing that gifts "grow their power and your bond,
unlocking new abilities" — plural, and tied to bond.

## Considered options

Deriving the glaze from the entity's domain combined with the tile's clay was
rejected: it is cheaper to author and more combinatorial, but it turns entities into
interchangeable reagents, and the pet-raising pillar (Chao Garden, Viva Piñata) needs
them to be characterful individuals you bond with.

## Consequences

Content cost is an ability list per entity. A glaze can carry effects as strong as
*meld without adjacency*, which makes forcing a meld something a player earns by
bonding rather than a starting tile everyone holds.
