# Avatar and starting kit are separate choices

Character creation makes two independent picks. The **avatar** is purely visual — a
look and a skybox palette, chosen because the player likes the art, carrying no
mechanical weight at all. The **kit** is a separate pick from six, one per domain,
carrying typed starting furniture and designs, fruit, seeds, and early reward
weighting.

An earlier draft bound the two together, one domain per avatar, described as "mostly
cosmetic". Splitting them makes that literally true, and it dissolves the Bogle
problem: a shapeshifter fox needed a wildcard rule only because avatars carried rules.

## Consequences

A kit **seeds the opening hours; it is not a class.** Nothing locks a player to their
starting domain — bags, furniture and friendships steer wherever they like. Twelve
avatars against six kits gives 72 openings from very little content.
