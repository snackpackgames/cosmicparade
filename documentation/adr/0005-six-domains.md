# Six domains

The domain roster is **Forest, Grassland, Wetland, Stone, Flame, Gale** (working
names). An earlier draft listed twelve, one per avatar; those collapsed — Forest
absorbed Boreal Forest, Grassland absorbed Steppe and Scrubland, Wetland absorbed Bog,
Tidepools and Deep Ocean.

Six is driven by ADR-0004. Because every fusion look is hand-authored, the roster size
sets the art budget: six domains make **every** possible overlap authorable — 15
pairs, 20 triples, up to a single six-way, 57 combinations in all. Twelve domains give
286 counting only pairs and triples, which forces a generic fallback for most
combinations and so undercuts the authored-palette decision entirely.

## Consequences

Fusion arity needs no special rule: three-way and higher overlaps are all affordable,
so fusion can be capped anywhere or left uncapped. Six also matches the hex's six
sides. All twelve avatars survive as art — see ADR-0012.
