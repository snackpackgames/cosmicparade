# The parade is the only unit of time

Offerings last a number of parades, entity visits last a number of parades, plants grow
over parades. **Nothing advances while the player is in their realm.** Decorating,
rearranging, replanting and watching your friends are free; the clock ticks only when
the player chooses to sail out.

This is a deliberate deviation from the influences. Chao Garden and Viva Piñata both
run real clocks, and both generate real anxiety about leaving the game. Cosmic Parade's
stated goal is a garden players "want to decorate and hang out in" — a realm that
punishes hanging around cannot be that.

## Consequences

*Day* is retired as a unit; earlier notes measuring entity visits in days mean parades.
This removes idle **decay**, not idle **motion**: the shifting skybox and roaming
entities animate continuously as cosmetic life, decoupled from the clock.
