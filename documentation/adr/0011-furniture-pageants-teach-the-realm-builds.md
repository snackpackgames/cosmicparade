# Furniture: pageants teach, the realm builds

Pageant rewards drop **designs** for new furniture; the pieces themselves are built
from realm materials — wood from the fruit trees you grow, clay, dye. Once a design is
known it can be built again freely.

Furniture is what projects domain influence, so its supply paces the entire realm half
of the game. Making furniture reward-only would gate the cozy sandbox behind
competitive play, which fights the first pillar named in the design. Making it purely
craftable would thin pageant loot to nothing. Splitting knowledge from labour keeps the
realm self-sufficient while parading still governs variety.

## Consequences

Needs a design/blueprint inventory. Closes the orchard loop: trees grown for offerings
supply wood for the furniture that grows the trees. A name for "design" is still
open — *cartoon* is the mosaicist's term for a preparatory drawing.
